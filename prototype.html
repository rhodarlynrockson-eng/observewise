Practise writing an observation

Use this structured form to practise describing what happened, identifying learning and planning next steps.

Use fictional information only. Do not enter real children's names or personal information.

Observation details
What happened?

Describe actions and events. Keep assumptions separate from what you observed.

Include exact spoken words where relevant. If there were no spoken words, you may describe observed gestures or expressions. Do not invent a quotation.

Areas of learning and development (optional)

These areas follow the EYFS in England. Select any supported by your fictional observation. For other UK frameworks, leave these unchecked and describe relevant areas in the learning field below.

Learning and next steps

Explain possible skills or understanding using evidence from your fictional observation. Keep interpretation separate from description.
